About this open archive, updates, and privacy

Learning Unfolded is the lecture series within FieldExplorer. Only selected public, general-purpose teaching lectures are included. Course-module and assignment walkthroughs, private videos, and other channel materials are excluded.

Guides and Korean summaries are editorial aids based on the supplied captions and linked sources. Further reading is selected by the editor, not necessarily cited in the video. Access and verification notes distinguish abstracts, excerpts, and relevant full-text passages. Illustrative or simulated examples are not empirical study findings. Public access does not itself grant reuse or modification rights.

The archive has no account system or research-data collection script. Opening a lecture loads a YouTube or Google Drive player. YouTube's player API is loaded on YouTube lecture pages to support seeking and timestamp capture. The video hosts, font provider, and hosting provider may process connection data under their own policies. Caption downloads are generated locally in your browser.

Problem reports are prepared locally. “Review report on GitHub” opens a public issue form and requires GitHub sign-in; nothing is submitted automatically. Do not include personal learner information, confidential research data, or credentials.
