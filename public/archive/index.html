About the content & your privacy

Lecture summaries are editorial guides based on the videos’ published descriptions, not transcripts. Topic categories are curated navigation, not formal prerequisites.

This static portal has no sign-in, tracking script, or research-data collection code. Opening a lecture loads an embedded YouTube or Google Drive player within this archive. Playback begins when you press play. Those services and the hosting provider may process standard connection data under their own policies.

Catalog checked October 16, 2026. Only selected public, general-purpose teaching lectures are included; course-module and assignment walkthroughs, private videos, and other channel materials are excluded.
