Behind the archive

For people who
care how we learn.

The content archive is part of FieldExplorer, curated by Jewoong Moon, connecting learning sciences with the decisions we make as teachers, designers, and researchers.

Its starting point is a collection of short teaching lectures. Interactive companions make selected ideas tangible. The aim is not to replace a full course or a primary source, but to create a clearer way into both.

The explorable format is inspired by Seeing Theory. These are original learning-science activities, not a reproduction of that project.

About the content & your privacy

Lecture summaries are editorial guides based on the videos’ published descriptions, not transcripts. Category assignments and suggested paths are curated navigation, not formal prerequisites. Illustrative models are labeled and should not be interpreted as evidence about real learners.

This static portal has no sign-in, tracking script, or research-data collection code. Playground interactions run in your browser. Videos connect to YouTube only when you choose to load one or open a YouTube link. YouTube and the hosting provider may process standard connection data under their own policies.

Catalog checked October 16, 2026. Only the 30 recent public teaching lectures are included; private videos and other channel materials are excluded.
