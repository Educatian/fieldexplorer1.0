# Archive review labels

Creator Confirmed records authorship and consent to archive inclusion for the listed lecture ID. KELS Curator Reviewed and KELS Board Approved are separate confirmations with their own scopes. None is an automatic scientific-quality grade.

Only a complete, explicitly confirmed record with a name, actual confirmation date, scope and reference receives a badge. Missing, pending, withdrawn or incomplete records do not. The October 28 creator statement covers its original 72 IDs; later releases require their own explicit confirmation. Checks report the remaining queue without manufacturing approval.

Maintainers: regenerate `docs/archive-creator-confirmation.md` with `node scripts/check_archive.mjs --review-queue`. Do not store private correspondence or personal contact details in public records.
